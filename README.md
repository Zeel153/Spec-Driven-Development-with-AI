## Reflection

For this project, I created a personal portfolio website by following a step-by-step AI workflow instead of trying to build everything at once. I started by exploring different design ideas, portfolio sections, and simple website structures. After comparing the options, I chose a clean and professional design that would make my work easy to read and understand.

Next, I planned the main parts of the website, including the homepage, about section, projects, skills, and contact information. I used AI to help organize my ideas, improve my wording, and check that my website matched the assignment goals. I also worked in small steps, testing each part before moving to the next one. This helped me avoid mistakes and made the project easier to manage.

One important thing I learned is that AI works best when I give it clear instructions and specific goals. I also learned how useful it is to create a plan, follow a checklist, and make small improvements instead of changing everything at once. Overall, this project helped me understand how to turn a vague idea into a finished website.
